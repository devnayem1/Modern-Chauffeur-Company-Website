FAQ

Common Questions
Answered

How far in advance should I book? +

We recommend booking at least 24 hours in advance, though we can often accommodate last-minute requests. For weddings and large events, please book as early as possible.

Do you offer fixed-price quotes? +

Yes. All our quotes are fixed-price with no hidden charges. The price you receive is the price you pay — no surge pricing, no surprises.

What happens if my flight is delayed? +

We monitor all incoming flights in real-time. If your flight is delayed, your chauffeur will adjust accordingly at no extra charge. You'll never be waiting and never be charged for delays outside your control.

Can I request a specific vehicle? +

Absolutely. You can specify your preferred vehicle class when submitting your quote request, and we'll do our best to accommodate your preference.

Are child seats available? +

Yes, we can provide child seats upon request. Please indicate this when completing your quote form and we will ensure an appropriate seat is fitted prior to your journey.

Is there a waiting charge? +

For airport pickups, we include a complimentary waiting period of 60 minutes after the scheduled landing. For all other services, waiting time is charged at a transparent per-minute rate.

Do you offer corporate accounts? +

Yes. We offer tailored corporate accounts with monthly invoicing, priority booking, and dedicated account managers. Contact us to discuss a package suited to your business needs.

What areas do you cover? +

We primarily operate across Greater London and all major UK airports, but can accommodate long-distance and nationwide journeys upon request.
